## Format and mobile-reference outcome

All thirty-five uploaded assets are landscape-oriented design boards or multi-page composites. There are **no mobile-only source assets** in this collection. The public experience therefore uses consistent browser-style framing for curated desktop concept boards; no phone-frame treatment is required, and no supplied source is being misrepresented as a mobile-only preview.

## Public curation rule

Every displayed concept is drawn from a board that visibly contains a coherent three-page set. The portfolio does not promote an incomplete family as complete. Asset page tabs create focused views of the matching pages already visible in the selected source board; no fictional client page has been manufactured.
